# Verdant Roots Showcase

give me an e commerce website that with
Build a premium, modern, mobile-first frontend website prototype for a Sri Lankan plant nursery and agricultural products business.

IMPORTANT:
This is a CLIENT DEMO / SALES PROTOTYPE for a real business owner. The goal is to make the owner immediately feel:
1. “This website fits my business.”
2. “My customers could easily find my plants and products.”
3. “This looks trustworthy and professional.”
4. “These developers understand my business, not just website design.”

Do NOT build a generic corporate website.
Do NOT make it look like a template.
Do NOT make it overly futuristic or overly animated.
Do NOT add fake reviews, fake awards, fake certifications, fake customer numbers, fake years of experience, or fake business claims.

The design should feel authentic to a Sri Lankan plant nursery / agriculture business.
